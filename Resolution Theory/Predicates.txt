
### Core Predicates (revised)

**1. Resolution**  
\[
\operatorname{Res}(g^{(\ell)}, \ell)
\]  
\(g^{(\ell)}\) is a Riemannian metric (or metric-like tensor field) at discrete resolution level \(\ell \in \{0,\dots,8\}\).

**2. Projection**  
\[
\Pi_{\ell_1 \to \ell_2}(g^{(\ell_1)}, g^{(\ell_2)})
\]  
\(g^{(\ell_2)}\) is obtained from \(g^{(\ell_1)}\) by the Gaussian (or more general) coarse-graining map with \(\ell_1 > \ell_2\).

**3. High-Density Node**  
\[
\operatorname{HDN}(g^{(\ell)}, x)
\]  
At point \(x\), the curvature invariants (or \(\det g\), Kretschmann, etc.) exceed a threshold at level \(\ell\). These are the black-hole / information-density peaks.

**4. Trauma Point**  
\[
\operatorname{TP}(g^{(\ell)}, t, x)
\]  
A high-density node that is *dynamically active* as an attractor in the potential at time \(t\).  
(Every trauma point is an HDN, but not every HDN need be currently traumatic.)

**5. Potential**  
\[
V^{(\ell)}(x,t)
\]  
The scale-dependent potential that generates the force \(-\nabla V^{(\ell)}\).

**6. Flow**  
\[
\operatorname{Flow}(x(t), g^{(\ell)}, V^{(\ell)})
\]  
The trajectory \(x(t)\) obeys the over-damped (or geodesic + force) equation defined by the current metric and potential.

**7. Coherence**  
\[
\operatorname{Coh}(g^{(\ell)}, t, U)
\]  
On a region \(U\), the metric is close to a reference (flatter / higher-symmetry) metric, or the particle flow is aligned with the local geodesics of steepest descent.

**8. Bias**  
\[
\beta_{ij}^{(\ell)}(x,t) = g_{ij}^{(\ell)}(x,t) - g_{ij}^{(\ell)\text{ref}}(x)
\]  
(or the normalized version you had earlier). This is a *field*, not merely a predicate; the predicate form can be \(\operatorname{Biased}(g^{(\ell)}, x, t)\) when \(\|\beta\|\) exceeds a threshold.

**9. Healing Process**  
\[
\operatorname{HP}(g^{(\ell)}, t_1, t_2, U)
\]  
Between \(t_1\) and \(t_2\), on region \(U\), either:  
- the depth of a trauma well decreases, or  
- the residual curvature after projection decreases, or  
- coherence on \(U\) increases.

### Useful Derived / Relational Predicates

- \(\operatorname{Residual}(g^{(\ell)}, x)\) — an HDN or TP that survives projection to level \(\ell\).  
- \(\operatorname{Trapped}(x(t), \ell)\) — the trajectory remains inside a trauma basin at that resolution.  
- \(\operatorname{Escaped}(x(t), \ell_1, \ell_2)\) — the particle was trapped at \(\ell_1\) but is no longer trapped after projection to \(\ell_2 < \ell_1\).

### Example Formalizations (cleaned)

**Initial sharp trauma**  
\[
\operatorname{TP}(g^{(8)}, t_0, x_0) \land \operatorname{HDN}(g^{(8)}, x_0)
\]

**Persistence under coarse-graining**  
\[
\operatorname{TP}(g^{(8)}, t_0, x_0) \land \Pi_{8\to 4}(g^{(8)}, g^{(4)}) \;\implies\; \operatorname{Residual}(g^{(4)}, x_0)
\]

**Healing at fine scale restores coherence**  
\[
\operatorname{HP}(g^{(8)}, t_1, t_2, U) \land t_3 > t_2 \;\implies\; \operatorname{Coh}(g^{(8)}, t_3, U)
\]

**Resolution-dependent escape (the key phenomenon the visualizer shows)**  
\[
\operatorname{Trapped}(x(t), 7) \land \Pi_{7\to 3}(g^{(7)}, g^{(3)}) \;\land\; \operatorname{Escaped}(x(t'), 7, 3)
\]  
for some \(t' > t\).
